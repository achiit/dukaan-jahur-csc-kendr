Two years of helping neighbours go digital

I started this centre two years ago because I saw people from Phudkipur losing a full day, and the bus fare, just to fill one form in the town. Most of them were not careless. They simply did not have anyone to explain the online process.

Today, many families here come to me for their Aadhaar, PAN, scholarship and scheme forms. I check every document before I submit, so you do not have to return for the same work twice.

If I do not know how to do something, I will say so and find out. That is how trust gets built in a small place.

  • One place for Government and non-government online work
